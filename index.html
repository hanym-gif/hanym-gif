Academic homepage

Theoretical and computational condensed-matter physics

I work on topological band structures, Berry curvature, Hall response, and quantum transport in finite lattice systems. My current numerical work connects bulk band features with ribbon spectra and transport observables.

Topological quantum matter

Band topology, Chern transitions, Berry-flux diagnostics, and valley-resolved gap closing in graphene and Haldane-type systems.

Quantum transport

Finite-geometry conductance, Hall response, edge states, dephasing probes, and numerical workflows for parameter sweeps.

Research interests

  • Topological phases and Chern transitions in lattice models
  • Berry curvature, anomalous Hall response, and Kubo formulas
  • Quantum transport in graphene and Haldane-type systems
  • Edge states, ribbon spectra, and finite-size effects
  • Reproducible numerical workflows for physics simulations
